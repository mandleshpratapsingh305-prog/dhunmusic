## Features ✨️    
- Play music from YouTube Music / YouTube ad-free and in the background
- Three Now Playing styles: Classic, Material 3 Expressive, and Modern Player
- Ten-band equalizer with presets and AutoEq headphone profiles
- On-device listening analytics: charts, period history, listening clock
- Word-by-word synced lyrics, romanization support, and share lyrics as an image
- Home screen widgets: turntable, playlists, and listening insights
- High-quality audio streaming (up to 256kbps Opus/AAC)
- Browse Home, Charts, Moods & Genres with fast caching
- Full search functionality for tracks, artists, albums, and playlists
- Spotify Canvas support
- Import playlists converted from Spotify and other music platforms
- Caching and offline playback support
- Customizable UI themes (Light, Dark, Dynamic Material Colors)
- Sleep Timer, Android Auto support, and Discord Rich Presence

## Data & Services
- Utilizes YouTube Music endpoints for fast, accurate audio playback and search metadata.
- Spotify Web API integration for Canvas artwork and lyric formatting.
- Integrated with SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike for enhanced playback control.
- Lyrics powered by LRCLIB and synchronized lyric providers.

---

## 🔒 Privacy
DhunMusic does not contain any third-party ad trackers. It connects directly to public APIs to retrieve music streams and metadata. Your personal listening data remains stored locally on your device.
